Result interpretation

What we learned, and what we can't claim yet.

Findings
    Limits & ethics
    • The cohort is synthetic; real responses so far are 15 high performers with no outcome label.
    • Self-reported answers carry bias; correlation is not causation.
    • Semester history comes from the generator; the form does not yet ask for per-semester SGPA.
    • A risk score must only trigger an offer of support, never a label or a penalty.
