Findings
    Limits & next steps
    • The cohort is synthetic: its generator builds answers additively from a few latent traits, which suits logistic regression.
    • 400 students and 15 features: small data limits how much complex ensembles can learn.
    • Real responses with outcome labels may contain interactions where trees and boosting win.
    • Prefer the simpler model when scores tie: logistic regression or BMA are easier to explain to a student.
